Yozgat

A self hosted deployment platform built with restraint. Every decision favors clarity, maintenance, and longevity over novelty.

Principles

  • Quiet before expressive.
  • Readable before stylish.
  • Stable before exciting.
  • Simple before clever.
  • Built to age gracefully.
  • Visual confidence through consistency.
Trust comes from predictability, not decoration.

Interaction

  • Fast transitions under 150ms.
  • No bouncing animations.
  • No floating elements.
  • No glass effects.
  • Small radius between 6px and 8px.
  • Thin borders instead of heavy shadows.
  • Whitespace defines hierarchy.
Yozgat is not rustic or nostalgic. It is modern because it removes everything that does not help the operator accomplish their work.
